Lead-to-cash demo reel

35–40 min Prereq: Modules 2–4, stock on hand Output: unlisted video + script

Demand theme

Hire pages and freelancer profiles for Odoo on Upwork cluster around CRM + Sales + Inventory + Invoicing as one integrated flow. Catalog services advertise “Sales pipelines: quotations … instant delivery order generation.” Jobs tell applicants to send a portfolio. A silent PDF of a kanban is weak. A 4-minute path from lead to paid invoice is what a buyer can forward to their operations person.

You are not claiming a conversion rate. You are showing you can operate the product they named.

Constraints

  • Database: primer_portfolio, demo data off.
  • One customer (Northwind), one contact (Maya), one SKU (beans) already in stock from EX01 or a quick receipt.
  • Leads enabled so the first 30 seconds are not “we skip qualification.”
  • No music. No intro logo. Cursor large. Cut silence.
  • Record with whatever is free: OBS, browser recorder, phone pointed at the screen if you must. Loom’s free tier is allowed if you already have it; this primer does not require a paid Loom plan — a listed script plus screenshots still counts if video is blocked.
  • Script printed or in a second monitor. You rehearsed once without recording.
  • Take recorded in one pass or two cuts max. Filename harborline-lead-to-cash.mp4.
  • portfolio/lead-to-cash/ contains script, 6 PNGs, and a link or the video file (git-lfs not required; do not commit a huge mp4 if GitHub complains — link unlisted instead).

Talk track (about 4:00)

0:00 This is a lab database, Odoo 18 Community, company Harborline.
     Path: lead → opportunity → quotation → order → delivery → invoice → payment.

0:20 CRM: Leads are on. New lead, Maya at Northwind, 20 bags of beans,
     expected revenue 840. Convert to opportunity, link the company contact.
     Activity: call tomorrow. That is how a manager sees follow-up.

1:10 New quotation from the opportunity. Product HB-BEAN-25, qty 20,
     invoicing policy delivered quantities. Preview. Confirm — it is now
     a sales order, not a second document.

2:00 Inventory: delivery smart button, stock reserved from the receipt
     we did earlier. Validate. On-hand drops. Stock move Stock → Customer.

2:40 Create invoice. It is allowed because we delivered. Confirm.
     Journal items: receivable, income, tax if any. Register payment, bank.
     Invoice paid.

3:20 Back on the opportunity: quotations/orders smart buttons, marked Won.
     What I need from a real client: stages they actually use, who may
     confirm orders, and tax owner. End.

Done when

  • A non-Odoo friend can tell you the six steps after watching once.
  • You say “lab database” in the first ten seconds.
  • The script file is in git even if the video is not.

What to show on Upwork

  • Profile: a portfolio item titled “Odoo 18 Community — lead to cash (lab)” with the six-step strip.
  • Proposal: first sentence names their modules; second sentence offers a 4-minute walkthrough of this path on their staging DB; third sentence links the unlisted video if the job asked for samples.
  • Do not auto-play video in the proposal text dump. Link it. Many buyers skim.
  • Screenshot filenames: 01-lead.png … 06-paid-invoice.png.
