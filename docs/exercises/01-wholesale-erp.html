Company brief (lab fiction)

Harborline Wholesale exports dry goods from Oakland. One warehouse, one-step receipts and deliveries. Sells 25kg commodity bags. Buys from importers. Wants:

  • Customers and vendors as companies, with one buyer contact each
  • Storable products with cost and list price, invoice on delivery
  • PO → receipt → on-hand → SO → delivery → invoice → payment
  • A reordering rule on the hero SKU
  • Payment terms Net 30 on customers

Out of scope: lots, multi-warehouse, customs, Studio, website. Custom documents are Exercise 03.

Session checklist

  • Company renamed Harborline Wholesale. Currency USD. Apps: Sales, CRM (optional), Inventory, Purchase, Invoicing. Developer mode on.
  • Partners: Northwind Grocers (customer), Andean Coffee Export or Lumen Packaging (vendor), Maya Chen as contact. Products: HB-BEAN-25, HB-RICE-20, SVC-ONB. Use setup/sample-data/ CSVs or type them. Categories have income accounts.
  • PO 80 bags beans → receive. SO 20 bags to Northwind → deliver → invoice on delivered qty → register payment. Reordering rule min 40 max 120. Optional: second SO that is only partially deliverable to talk about allocation.
  • Folder portfolio/harborline-erp/ with numbered PNGs and README.md (4-sentence story + what is lab).

Partial allocation talking point

The wholesale job wanted allocation from on-hand and incoming. In standard Odoo, forecasted qty includes incoming POs; a confirmed SO reserves on-hand. Show: PO confirmed not yet received, SO confirmed, forecast vs on-hand. Do not fake a custom allocator in this sitting.
