Module 04

Accounting basics for implementers

You are not the client’s CPA. You are the person who makes invoices post, taxes land on the right account, and payments mark invoices paid. This sitting is conceptual plus a Community Invoicing lab. Stay honest about Enterprise.

45 min Prereq: Modules 1–3 Produces: posted invoice + payment

Why this shows up on Upwork

Wholesale and “full ERP” jobs name Accounting and Invoicing next to Sales and Inventory. They ask for invoices from sales orders/deliveries, payment status and aging, vendor bills from purchase orders, chart of accounts, taxes. Implementer posts treat Accounting as a module to configure, not as a five-year finance transformation. Your portfolio should show a posted customer invoice, a vendor bill, and a payment — with the journal items visible — not a screenshot of the dashboard gadget.

Community Invoicing vs Enterprise Accounting

The official Docker image is Community. Community includes the Invoicing app (account): charts via localization, customer invoices, vendor bills, credit notes, taxes, journals, payments, basic reports. Enterprise Accounting adds a lot of the product marketing: bank sync, the polished reconciliation workspace, assets, follow-up letters, consolidations, some analytic/budget features, and Studio on top.

Odoo.sh is Enterprise. If a job requires Studio screens or bank-feed setup, say so in the proposal and price the edition correctly. Do not install random “accounting kit” third-party modules on a portfolio database you cannot explain.

In modern Odoo, invoices are journal entries (account.move) with a move type. Confirming an invoice is posting a move. That is why “delete this invoice” after post is the wrong request; you credit note it.

Product accounts

Income and expense accounts usually come from the product category, not each product. Implementers who set accounts on 400 products by hand make upgrades miserable. Category “Saleable” → income 400000-ish, expense/COGS as the localization defines.

Inventory valuation (do not pretend)

If stock is automated into the ledger, every receipt and delivery (or, in Odoo 19, invoice-time perpetual) posts extra journal items. Wrong valuation accounts = month-end nightmare. For Community labs, leave valuation manual unless you are following a finance owner’s written choice. You still set product cost so margin reports are not fiction.

Order of configuration (steal this for proposals)

  1. Localization / company / currency
  2. Chart of accounts tweaks
  3. Taxes and fiscal positions
  4. Journals and payment terms
  5. Product categories (accounts)
  6. Then Sales / Inventory documents

Turning on Sales before taxes exist is how demo invoices post with 0% and nobody notices until week four.

Lab

1. Open the chart (8 min)

Invoicing (or Accounting) → Configuration → Chart of Accounts. Filter type Receivable, then Income, then Expense. Find the default income account your beans product will use: product category → Accounting tab (developer mode helps). Note one receivable account and one bank journal (Configuration → Journals).

Company form: currency, country. If you picked United States at database create, you have a US-ish chart and tax lines. If you picked a generic or other country, do not mix screenshots as “US GAAP setup.”

  • You wrote down: receivable account code, income account code, bank journal name.

2. Tax on the product (5 min)

Open HB-BEAN-25. Customer taxes: pick a sales tax from the localization (or none if you are modeling wholesale tax-exempt — then say that out loud). Fiscal position on Northwind: if they are a reseller, you may map tax to 0%. For the lab, a simple sales tax is clearer on the invoice PDF.

  • You can explain why the invoice tax line is that amount (rate × base).

3. Customer invoice from the SO (10 min)

If Module 3 delivered the beans, Create Invoice on the SO should offer a regular invoice for 20 bags. Create and post (Confirm). Open the invoice → Journal Items (developer mode or the journal items tab). You want to see: debit receivable, credit income, tax lines if any.

If the SO cannot invoice: invoicing policy is delivery and delivery is not done — go finish Module 3, or invoice the service product SVC-ONB as a standalone invoice: Invoicing → Customers → Invoices → New.

  • Posted customer invoice. Journal items screenshot saved.

4. Vendor bill (8 min)

If you had a PO for 100 bags, Create Bill from the PO after (or before, depending on bill-on-receipt policy) and post it. Otherwise: Invoicing → Vendors → Bills → New, vendor Andean, one line beans qty 100, price 28, post.

Read the items: debit expense or stock-related account depending on configuration, credit payable. You do not need to win a debate with an accountant today; you need to see payable go up.

  • Posted vendor bill with a payable line you can point at.

5. Payment (8 min)

On the customer invoice: Register Payment. Journal: Bank. Amount: full. Post. Invoice payment state should become Paid (or In Payment, depending on version and whether you still need to reconcile a bank statement). Community without bank rec still lets you register a payment against the invoice.

Optional: register a partial payment to see residual amount. Aging: Invoicing → Reporting → Partner Ledger or Aged Receivable if present. Screenshot.

  • Customer invoice shows paid/in payment. You did not “just change the state” in a hack.

Never

Never delete posted moves. Never give sales users Accountant rights “so they can fix it.” Never go live without a finance person signing the tax mapping. You can still be the implementer who prepares the chart and records the session.

Recall prompts

What model is a posted invoice?

account.move with type out_invoice. Lines are account.move.line.

A client on Community asks for Odoo Studio to add a field on the invoice. Your answer?

Studio is Enterprise. On Community you add a small custom module (Module 5) or they buy Enterprise.

Why configure taxes before the first real invoice?

Posted invoices freeze tax lines. Fixing history is credit notes and embarrassment. Product categories and fiscal positions need to be right first.

Invoice-on-delivery vs invoice-on-order — which wholesale job is that?

Physical goods: usually delivered quantities so you do not bill what you could not ship. Services: ordered quantities.

Where should the default income account live?

Product category accounting fields, overridden on the product only when one SKU is special.

Done when

  • You posted a customer invoice and can read its journal items.
  • You posted a vendor bill.
  • You registered a payment and the invoice is no longer “not paid.”
  • You can say what Community does not include (Studio, sh, full Accounting suite) without sounding bitter.

What to show on Upwork

  • Screenshot trio: invoice form, journal items, paid banner.
  • One sentence on localization: “US chart from database creation; taxes reviewed with their bookkeeper.”
  • Do not title the portfolio piece “Full Odoo Accounting implementation” if you only posted invoices on Community. Title it “Quote-to-cash invoicing and payments (Community)” or “Invoicing setup for Harborline lab.”
  • If the job is Enterprise, say you will configure on their sh/staging, not that your Docker Community file is their ledger.
