Module 02

CRM + Sales pipeline

Clients say “set up CRM and Sales.” They mean: a place for messy interest, a kanban of deals, a quotation that becomes an order without retyping the customer. You will run that path once, slowly, and name every object.

40 min Prereq: Module 1 Produces: one won deal with a SO

Why this shows up on Upwork

Implementation posts list CRM and Sales as core modules next to Inventory and Accounting. Catalog services sell “Sales pipelines: quotations, price lists, discount tiers, delivery order generation.” A wholesale distribution job on Upwork described sales orders that allocate from on-hand and incoming stock — that is Sales talking to Inventory, which you start here and finish in Module 3.

If you can only show a kanban full of demo cards named “Interest in your products,” you look like a trial user. If you can show this Harborline lead becoming a quotation with real product codes, you look like an implementer.

Objects on the path

Contactres.partnerCustomer. Companies and people. Do not duplicate blindly.

Leads and opportunities are the same model with a type flag. That is why conversion feels like a rename plus a pipeline stage. Quotations and orders are the same model with a state. Confirming a quote does not copy it into a second document; it changes state and unlocks stock and invoicing.

Settings that change the product

  • CRM → Configuration → Settings → Leads. If this is off, you only see Opportunities. Many “our CRM is confusing” tickets are this checkbox.
  • Sales teams and pipeline stages: CRM → Configuration → Sales Teams / Stages. Default stages (New, Qualified, Proposition, Won) are enough for the lab.
  • Sales → Settings → Quotation templates and Online signature / payment: useful later, skip today.
  • Invoicing policy on the product: ordered quantities vs delivered quantities. Wholesale jobs almost always want delivered quantities for physical goods.

Lab — one Harborline deal

Use primer_learn. If you already have a clean primer_portfolio, you may repeat the same clicks there after this sitting.

1. Turn on Leads (3 min)

CRM → Configuration → Settings → enable Leads → Save. You should see a Leads menu. If you skip this, the rest of the lab still works from an opportunity; you will just miss a question clients ask.

  • Leads menu exists. You created lead Northwind — Q4 beans trial with expected revenue 1,200, partner name Maya Chen, company Northwind Grocers, notes: “wants 20 bags organic black beans, net 30, Oakland dock.”

2. Qualify (5 min)

Open the lead. Click Convert to Opportunity. Create the customer if it does not exist (company Northwind Grocers, person Maya Chen linked to it). Set the sales team. After conversion, find it on CRM → Pipeline.

Drag the card: New → Qualified. Schedule an activity: Call, due tomorrow, “confirm pallet count.” Activities are what managers mean by “make salespeople use the CRM.”

  • Opportunity on the kanban with an activity. Chatter shows the conversion.

3. Quotation from the opportunity (10 min)

On the opportunity, New Quotation. Confirm the customer is Northwind, not a duplicate individual. Add a line:

  • If demo data is on, pick a storable product you will also use in Inventory. If not, create product HB-BEAN-25 Organic Black Beans 25kg, type Storable, sales price 42, cost 28, invoicing policy Delivered quantities.
  • Quantity 20.

Set payment terms if the localization loaded any (30 days). Optional: Sales → Products → Pricelists — skip unless you have time; mention them in the recall.

Send the quotation (or “Preview”). State becomes Quotation Sent if you mark it sent. Note the smart buttons on the opportunity: Quotations = 1.

  • Quotation SO00xx linked from the opportunity, 20 × beans, customer is the company.

4. Confirm the order (5 min)

Confirm. The banner should read Sales Order. A Delivery smart button appears if the product is storable and Inventory is installed. Do not validate the delivery yet if you have zero stock — Module 3 is the stock sitting. For this module, confirming is enough. If you already have on-hand qty from demo data, you may peek at the delivery form and leave it.

Mark the opportunity Won. The CRM and the order are now two views of the same win. That linkage is the whole point of using Odoo instead of a spreadsheet plus a separate quote tool.

  • Order state is Sales Order. Opportunity is Won. You can jump opportunity → quotation → order without search.

5. Optional in this sitting: invoice (5 min)

If the product invoicing policy is ordered quantities, Create Invoice works immediately. If it is delivered quantities, Odoo will refuse until you deliver — which is correct, and a sentence you should use on calls. Either create a service line to invoice now, or stop and wait for Module 3.

  • You either posted an invoice, or you wrote in your notes: “beans are invoice-on-delivery; blocked until stock move is done.” Both count.

Gotchas implementers hit in week one

Duplicate partners

Converting a lead with a free-typed name creates a new res.partner. Sales then cannot see history. Always search the customer field; merge duplicates under Contacts if you already made a mess.

Sales vs CRM menus

Quotations live in Sales. Pipeline lives in CRM. Clients will say “the quote is in CRM.” Train them with the smart buttons, not with a lecture.

Other landmines: confirming with a 0-price line; using a consumable when they needed stock; pricelists in USD while the company currency is something else; salespeople in the wrong sales team so the pipeline looks empty (filter “My Pipeline”).

Recall prompts

What Python model is a lead? An opportunity? A quotation?

Lead and opportunity: crm.lead. Quotation and sales order: sale.order. State/type flags, not separate tables.

A user says they have no Leads menu. First check?

CRM Settings → Leads checkbox. Then their access rights (Sales / User vs Administrator).

Why might Create Invoice be disabled on a confirmed order of storable products?

Product invoicing policy is delivered quantities and the delivery is not validated (or not fully delivered).

Name the six-step flow you would draw on a sales call.

Lead → Opportunity → Quotation → Sales order → Delivery → Invoice (then payment in Module 4).

Done when

  • You can point at one Harborline (or Northwind) deal and hop lead → opportunity → quotation → order.
  • You know whether Leads are enabled and why.
  • You can say in one sentence when Odoo will not invoice a physical product.
  • Screenshots: pipeline kanban with the card, quotation form with lines, confirmed SO header.
